Bio
Liqun is a PhD candidate in the School of Life and Environmental Sciences at the University of Sydney. He holds a Master of Environmental Science from the University of Sydney and completed his undergraduate studies in Environmental Science at Minzu University of China and University College Cork, Ireland. His research interests include precision agriculture, crop modelling, machine learning, spatial analysis, and sustainable agricultural management. His previous research focused on integrating APSIM-derived surrogate models with spatial optimisation to balance crop productivity and greenhouse gas emissions. His current doctoral research builds on this foundation, exploring data-driven approaches to support sustainable and economically efficient nitrogen management in cropping systems.
Research
Balancing Crop Yield and N₂O Mitigation through Spatial Optimisation of Nitrogen Management Using APSIM-Derived Surrogate Models.
My PhD research focuses on developing data-driven approaches to optimise nitrogen management in cropping systems, aiming to improve agricultural productivity and profitability while reducing environmental impacts. By integrating crop modelling, machine learning, spatial data analysis, and multi-objective optimisation, the research investigates spatial and temporal variability in crop responses to nitrogen fertilisation. It also explores the transferability of predictive models across fields and seasons to support site-specific nitrogen management. Ultimately, this research aims to develop practical decision-support approaches for more profitable and environmentally sustainable precision agriculture.
